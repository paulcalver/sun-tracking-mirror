Place & display

Pure elevation: the hand sits on the right and its angle from the horizontal is the elevation, so it swings up and back down each day. Elevation (flips) mode: the hand points at the sun's height above the horizon, on the left in the morning (east) and the right in the afternoon (west); it points down below the horizon (the line stays black). Sky view: the hand points at the sun's position projected on a south-facing wall, so it passes straight over the top without flipping. Azimuth is the compass bearing from the observer (north up, east right). All positions are as seen from Earth at the chosen place. Positions use low-precision solar and lunar formulas (about 0.1° sun, 0.3° moon, a few arcminutes for planets).
