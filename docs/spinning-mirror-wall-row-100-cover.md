# Clear cover for the 100 mm wall piece: options (2026-11-18)

Notes only, nothing modelled in the script or BOM, nothing built. Previews: `cad/media/previews/spinning-mirror-wall-row-100/cover-option1.png` (second rail and arms), `cover-floating-panel.png`, `cover-floating-panel-100border.png`.

## Sizes
- Piece: about 1302 mm wide (motor edge to motor edge) x 260 mm tall (electronics box bottom z = -44 to ring tops z = 216); rings reach 129 mm from the wall.
- Panel plane 145 mm from the wall (about 15 mm clear of the ring fronts; 160 mm would give about 30 mm).

## Preferred: floating front panel on standoffs
- Panel with a 100 mm border all round: about 1502 x 460 mm. Weight: 3 mm acrylic about 2.5 kg, 4 mm about 3.3 kg, 5 mm about 4.1 kg.
- Six stainless barrel standoffs (19 mm) at x = -211, 490, 1191 (piece coordinates, 50 mm in from the panel edges), z = -94 and 266. The bottom row is below the electronics box so nothing collides.
- The user's material is 3 mm anti-glare acrylic. On six points it is floppy (a firm push could reach the rings), so use 10 standoffs, five per row, 350 mm apart (x = -211, 140, 490, 840, 1191) and about 160 mm long. Standard sign standoffs are usually sold up to about 100 mm, so a 145 to 160 mm length may be custom or a tube over threaded rod.
- Anti-glare (etched) sheet scatters light passing through, so the mirrors behind may look hazy; get a sample and test it in front of a mirror first. True anti-reflective coated acrylic does not do this but costs much more.
- Wall: use proper plugs or studs for six to ten fixings plus the three rail standoffs.

## Alternative: second 2020 rail along the top with six printed arms
Rail at z = 226 to 246 on three more printed standoffs, panel bolted to both rails' front slots by six arms (65 mm reach from the front slot to the panel). Stiff and hides the fixings, and the top rail could carry an LED strip. Costs a second 1150 mm rail (about 13 GBP), six T-nuts and screws, three standoffs, six arms. The user found the preview busy, so this was not chosen.

## Not recommended
- A rail-only mount (one rail): the panel is about 290 mm tall and a single rail grips it at one height, so a push at the top would crack the acrylic around the holes.
- A full five-sided box: 4 to 5 kg, harder to make and service.
