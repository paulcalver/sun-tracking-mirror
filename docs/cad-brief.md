# CAD brief: hinged flat mirror, low bead hinge (current design, v4, built around real parts)

v4 drawn 2026-11-03 around the parts in `parts-sourcing.md`: 9 inch lazy Susan bearing, 20T motor pulley, 200 mm stock belt, Pico, Hall homing, laser-cut and printed parts. Source of truth: `cad/src/hinge_mirror.py` (CadQuery). Draft sizes, nothing printed or tested. Earlier passes are in git history; `cad/src/hinge_mirror_v1_exposed.py` is the first exposed-chain pass.

Parts list split by process: `bom-v4.md` / `bom/bom-v4.csv` (generated by `cad/src/make_bom.py`).

## Outputs

- `cad/out/hinge-mirror/parts/laser/*.dxf`: cut outlines of the laser parts, and `*_engrave.dxf` for the 2 mm locating pockets (underside pockets are mirrored: engrave with the sheet turned over about Y). Regenerated by `hinge_mirror.py`.
- `cad/media/animations/`: sun-tracking videos made by `cad/src/sun_seasons.py` (VTK + ffmpeg; 1080 x 1920, 4 days at 38.4 s, stacked and per day). - Re-run: `python3 cad/src/hinge_mirror.py [--tilt DEG] [--az DEG]` poses it; `--check` runs interference tests at tilt 0, 30, 62, 70 and both signs.

## Structure
- `base_fixed`: a black **cover** (6 mm top frame with a round 255 hole, glued to four 3 mm walls inset 3 mm, 74 mm tall, standing on the table, cable notch in the back wall) over a loose 287 mm square 6 mm floor centred by four printed corner locators, all laser cut. On the floor: 9 inch lazy Susan on six printed posts, printed fixed 80T GT2 hub, 22 mm slip ring with 44 mm flange (hub sits on the flange), azimuth home sensor.
- `drum_rotating` (turns about Z): 6 mm floor on the bearing, six printed pillars, 6 mm top plate (the lid), azimuth motor + 20T pulley + 200 mm belt (all inside the bearing bore), tilt motor + coupler + TR8x2 screw + end block, 4 mm guide rod (stops the yoke spinning with the screw) with a support block, Hall sensor on a column, two hinge end housings with 685 bearings, printed controller tray (Pico, DS3231, two TMC2209, buck converter), compass on a post.
  - `tilt_slider`: yoke block and brass nut, slides along Y by 40 sin(tilt). The yoke slot is open at the top, and a hole in the yoke slides on the guide rod.
  - `mirror_tilting` (origin on the hinge axis, revolute about X): 18 mm bar, two 5 mm stubs, raw 6 mm glass, crank arm and pin, home magnet.

## What shows from outside
A black square plinth with a round turntable flush in the top, a black 18 mm half round bar (9 mm proud, axis level with the top) with two black end housings, and the bare glass rising from the bar. Everything else is inside.

## Build method
- **Cover:** lift it off to reach the slip ring, wiring and ballast; it needs felt on the wall edges and the floor underside. **Glue seating:** printed parts and walls have a 2 mm tenon that glues into a 2 mm deep engraved pocket (0.1 mm clearance per side) in the laser plates: wall groove (frame underside only), bearing posts, drum pillars (both ends), controller tray legs, compass post, Hall column and the two tilt blocks. Engraving depth on ply or acrylic is only about +/-0.2 mm, so test on an offcut. Plates with underside pockets (top frame, drum top plate) have mirrored engrave files.
- **Laser cut (6 mm or thinner, ply or acrylic, no metal; cutter bed 1219 x 914, max 6 mm):** base floor (287 square, loose), four plain 3 mm wall panels (butt corners, glued into a groove in the frame, down to the table), top frame, drum floor, drum top plate (with a rectangular hole for the printed arm relief insert).
- **Printed on the P1S (256 x 256, PETG or ASA):** hub pulley (51 mm), bar (211 long), end housings, bearing posts, drum pillars, crank arm, yoke block, arm relief insert (fills the plate hole and has the sloped relief a laser cannot cut), screw end block, guide support block, controller tray (plate with four legs, heat-set insert holes for the floor screws and the Pico), compass post, Hall column.
- **Bought:** motors, bearing, slip ring, TR8x2 screw and nut, 685 and 688 bearings, 20T pulley, 200 mm GT2 loop, Pico, DS3231, TMC2209, buck converter, GY-271 compass, Hall boards, magnets, 5 mm rods, glass.

## Checks run (from the script)
- Interference: no clashes between any parts (all pairs) at tilt 0/45/-62/70 with the drum turned 0, 30, 75, 200 deg, and none at tilt 0, +/-30, +/-62, +/-70 in the `--check` runs.
- Gravity torque on the hinge, mirror flat: about 1.1 N.m. Lead screw force at 70 deg about 77 N; motor torque needed about 0.06 N.m against 0.255 N.m holding.
- **Stall hazard:** at full motor torque the nut can push about 320 N. Limit the driver current to 30 to 40 percent and fit end stops at 70 deg.
- Belt: 200 mm is a stock loop length (my listing search, UK shop not confirmed).

## Known approximations and open points
- **Bar stiffness:** a printed PETG bar may twist about 1 degree between the crank and the glass ends under the mirror's weight (my estimate, not checked). Fixes: arm nearer the centre, a stiffening rod, or an aluminium bar.
- The compass sits about 90 mm from the motors; expect to calibrate it in place and treat it as a coarse check only.
- The Pico's USB port faces the drum wall side; the lid lifts off with the mirror (the crank pin slides out of the open yoke slot).
- Bearing, slip ring flange and motor bolt patterns are not published; shaft length of the motors is assumed (24 mm). Pulleys are toothed, but the belt is a plain loop, the lead screw has no thread, and fasteners, brackets, wiring and the bar clamp are not modelled.
- Ballast (about 1.5 kg) is not modelled; space is free in the base corners. Mirror glass must stay flat. Table clearance: keep about 570 mm clear across for the mirror swing.
- Full +/-90 was considered and dropped. If wanted later: a cable wrapped on the bar pulled by a vertical screw, with the glass centred on an axis raised about 3 mm.

## Decisions since the v4 drawing (2026-11-03, all on main)
- Cover base: frame and walls glued as one cover that stands on the table; the floor sits loose inside, centred by four printed corner blocks. Acrylic in black for the box and the lid (wood was considered: birch ply, sand the charred edges, seal and oil; the design stays black acrylic).
- Parts are glued into engraved 2 mm pockets (tenons on the printed parts) instead of finger joints.
- Controller plate is a printed tray with heat-set inserts; plain NEMA 17 motors on both axes (no gearbox).
- Mirror back is black.

- Solid mirror with a 20 mm hole, sunlight patch and shadows on a white table (videos only). If wanted for real: add the hole to the CAD and the glass order, and check the hinge-bar end.
