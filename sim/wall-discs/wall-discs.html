Route 1b. Left to right: sun elevation, sun azimuth, moon elevation, moon azimuth. Each disc turns as a whole on its own hidden stepper and its inlaid black line is the hand. Elevation: line angle above the horizontal (right side). Azimuth: compass bearing, north up, east right. Tick X-ray to see the steppers and controller behind the panel. Drag to orbit a little, scroll to zoom.
