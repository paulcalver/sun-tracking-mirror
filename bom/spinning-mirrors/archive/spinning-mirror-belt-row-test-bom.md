## Not priced yet

- Row base: a 1 m rail (2020 extrusion with T-nuts and M5 screws, or printed mounts with slots) so each unit can slide to tension its belts.

- Printed parts: spindle bearing blocks, ring, carrier, hub for each unit (PETG or ASA).
- Already in the single-unit order and reused: NEMA 17 42/60, Adafruit TMC2209, ESP32, 24 V supply, fuse, capacitors, wiring.
- 1 mm foam mounting tape and neutral-cure silicone, if using glass.

## Check before ordering
- 625ZZ is 16 mm across, so the spindle bearing blocks are thicker than they would be with the MR105ZZ.
- Fit-test a 5 mm and a 3 mm rod in a bearing before cutting them all.
- The double-head pulley is about 28 mm long with the grub screw in the centre boss between the two belt channels; put a flat on the 5 mm spindle or use thread-lock.
