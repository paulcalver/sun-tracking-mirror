One dial, two faces. A hollow square frame stands flush on a round base and the whole frame turns to the sun's compass bearing (azimuth). A round window in each face holds a disc flush with the face. One GM2804 sits between the discs: disc 1 is on its rotor and disc 2 is carried on two posts round the motor, so both discs turn together about their centres and their inlaid lines point the same way in space. A fixed arm from the frame floor holds the motor. A second GM2804 stands in the base and turns the frame directly, with no gears; its centre is closed, so the cable runs down beside it and loops slack to a fixed socket (the loop is not drawn).
