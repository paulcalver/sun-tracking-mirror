Place & display

One dial. A deep square slab sits on a turntable and the whole square turns to the sun's compass bearing (azimuth). A white disc sits on each face, joined by an axle through the slab, and they turn together about their own centres so the inlaid black line shows the elevation from either side. Both steppers are in the base: one turns the turntable, the other turns a rod up through the hollow tube into the slab, where a 1:1 bevel pair drives the axle.
