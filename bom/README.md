# Bills of materials

The CSVs are generated by the CAD scripts in `cad/src/` (each script's docstring says which).

## Current
`spinning-mirrors/` holds the two live builds:
- `bom-spinning-mirror-wall-row-100.csv`: six 100 mm mirrors on one rail, a NEMA 17 at each end (extra column `qty_needed_8_mirror` gives the eight-mirror quantities).
- `bom-spinning-mirror-150-nema17.csv`: the single 150 mm test rig on a NEMA 17.

`sun-dials/` holds the sun dial BOMs (glow, one, small, GM2804 dials).

## Archive
Older builds, kept for reference; the scripts still write them, but into the archive folders.
- `spinning-mirrors/archive/`: GM2804 gimbal, row, test and trio; the 150 mm trio; the 50 mm wall rows (3 x 8 and the single row of ten); the belt-row test shopping list.
- `archive/finger-pointer/`, `archive/line-mirrors/`, `archive/arm-v4/`: the other projects (arm-v4 is the original hinged-mirror arm: v4 BOM from `cad/src/make_bom.py`, supplier list, stale UK draft).

New project: make a subfolder, point its script's `BOMF` there, add a row above.
